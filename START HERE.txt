Dig Duck 1.1.0 — local preview

Open Dig Duck.app.
Choose Save Folder, then allow read access to your Sneaky Sasquatch saves.
You can also choose Preview with example saves without selecting any files.

No Terminal, Homebrew, Node installation, or browser server is needed.
Your saves remain on your Mac and are never changed by Dig Duck.

This locally signed preview is not an App Store release. The App Store build
still needs distribution signing, validation, submission, and Apple approval.
