Dig Duck 1.0.1

How to run:

1. Double-click "Open Dig Duck.html".

2. Click "Choose Save Folder".

3. Choose the Sneaky Sasquatch save folder.

Dig Duck runs in your browser.
It reads the selected files on your Mac.
It does not upload your saves.

The save folder is usually here:

~/Library/Containers/com.rac7.SneakySasquatchMac/Data/Library/Application Support/com.rac7.SneakySasquatchMac
