# Dig Duck Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

This policy describes the native Mac app, version 1.1.0 and later. Version 1.1.0 is currently being prepared for release.

Dig Duck is a read-only Mac utility maintained by Michael Klepacz.

## Your saves stay on your Mac

Dig Duck reads the Sneaky Sasquatch save folder you choose through the macOS folder picker. It uses the supported save-slot files to display digsite progress and possible location matches. It does not modify your game saves or upload them to us or another service.

The app has no account system, advertising, analytics, or tracking. We do not collect personal information through the app.

## Remembering your choice

macOS can let Dig Duck remember read access to the selected folder. Dig Duck stores a security-scoped bookmark in its own local settings so you do not have to choose the folder on every launch. Choose **Forget Folder** to remove that bookmark. This does not delete your saves. Window placement may also be stored locally.

## External links and support

Guide and support links open your default browser only when you click them. Those websites have their own privacy policies and may receive ordinary web-request information. Dig Duck does not attach your save files to those requests.

If you choose to post a GitHub issue, the information you submit is handled by GitHub and may be public. Do not include private information or game saves unless you intentionally want to share them.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/mikeklepacz/dig-duck/issues without including sensitive information.
